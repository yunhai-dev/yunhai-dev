## A Short Intro

I'm **YunHai**, a developer who likes building software that feels simple on the surface and solid underneath.

My interests sit at the intersection of **web development**, **automation**, **developer tools**, and **small systems that solve real problems**. I care about clear structure, readable code, restrained design, and workflows that stay out of the user's way.

I use GitHub as a public record of growth: experiments, iterations, and half-formed ideas that get sharper through shipping.

## What I Like Building

- Personal tools that remove repetitive work.
- Web projects with clean interaction and strong structure.
- Small utilities that are easy to run, easy to read, and easy to extend.
- Projects that balance aesthetics, practicality, and maintainability.

## Current Direction

- Sharpening full stack development skills through real projects.
- Exploring modern frontend, scripting, and tool-driven workflows.
- Writing clearer code and better documentation over time.
- Building a body of work that reflects both taste and execution.
